Research Overview

My research focuses on the theory and application of Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Multimodal GenAI, and Brain-Inspired Spiking Neural Networks.

In fundamental research, I am particularly interested in creating intelligent algorithms that are both accurate and computationally efficient. In applied research, I am interested in bridging theory and real-world applications by leveraging various data modalities, including images, videos, and text.

On this page you can find the main themes of my research. Each theme will be expanded with ongoing projects, datasets, and publications. Please follow the research theme links below.
